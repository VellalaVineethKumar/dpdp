•	Frontend Access → Users interact through an intuitive Streamlit Web Interface with role-based access control, multi-language support, and responsive design for desktop/mobile access. Features include drag-and-drop functionality, real-time collaboration, and customizable dashboards.

•	Quality Layer → Data is validated for completeness, accuracy, consistency, and regulatory compliance before generating comprehensive reports and actionable recommendations with risk scoring and mitigation strategies. Includes automated validation rules and compliance checklists.
